04 / Beyond softwareResearch Expeditions

A question is only
the beginning.

Start with a question or explore directions suggested by the available data. The research workflow connects hypotheses, literature, datasets, analysis, reviews, and decisions.

01

Choose what to investigate.

An Idea Bloom develops possible directions. Models evaluate them before selected questions become bounded investigations.

02

Measure. Try to refute.

Analysis instruments work on real data. Experiments and checks test hypotheses rather than treating a persuasive answer as a finding.

03

Keep the whole record.

Results, disagreement, limitations, and unresolved claims travel with the evidence. Other researchers can inspect and build on the work.

Open an expedition ↗A published research record, not a claim of confirmed discovery.
