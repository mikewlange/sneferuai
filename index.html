Ask us something ↗
Is Sneferu a model or a coding assistant?+

Neither. It is the system around the models: planning, execution, tools, memory, review, and recovery. Software development is one use. Business creation, research, and game production use their own workflows on the same platform.

Why use different models?+

Models have different strengths and blind spots. Sneferu assigns them distinct responsibilities and uses independently trained model families to challenge the work. More voices are useful when disagreement improves the result—not just when they agree.

What do you mean by “finished”?+

The outcome has to meet the checks for that kind of work. A software build needs execution evidence; a research claim needs support. A completed run is not automatically a qualified product or a confirmed discovery. Remaining problems stay in the record.

Where can I see how it really works?+

The system overview explains the engines in plain language. The System Atlas and recorded field notes let you go deeper into the architecture and examples.

How can I work with Sneferu?+

Software On Demand is the live customer service for application builds. For the broader platform, research collaboration, or a partnership, talk to the founder about what you want to do.

Built with Sneferu / Infrastructure

ESM-Gateway.
Memory with a history.

Governed long-term memory for Sneferu—and other AI applications. A memory service that keeps track of what an entry means, where it came from, and how it changes.

More than saved text

Facts, preferences, decisions, and warnings are typed entries with provenance and confidence. Related memories can be retrieved as context for later work.

Change without amnesia

Conflict checks surface clashing claims. Supersession, review, and retirement let the system update what it remembers while preserving the trail.

Typed intake→Conflict checks→Governed storage→Recall

A separate memory service with a FastAPI interface and Postgres / pgvector storage. The diagram describes its architecture, not a live activity feed.
