Who this covers

Sneferu.ai, Inc. provides the Sneferu website and Software On Demand (SOD). This policy covers information we handle for visitors, people buying or using a private SOD node, and people contacting us.

If an organization provides your node or account, its administrator controls your access and the work you submit. That organization may have its own policies and responsibilities for personal information in its projects. A separate agreement may govern our processing of that project data.

Public Works is a shared service with different project visibility. This policy does not turn a Public Works project into a private node. Review the visibility controls and notices of the service you use.

Information we collect

  • Signup and account details: your name, email, invitation and account identifiers, password hashes, authentication records, and any promotion code you enter. If you use Google sign-in where available, we receive the identity information needed to authenticate you.
  • Orders and payments: the selected plan, price and discount, Stripe customer and subscription references, invoices, payment status, and usage records. You enter payment details on Stripe’s hosted checkout. Our signup form does not collect your full card number or card security code.
  • Project content: the requests, files, datasets, connected-source material, messages, code, specifications, outputs, and execution records you or your authorized users provide or generate. These may contain personal information you choose to include.
  • Service and security records: node and project identifiers, resource and model usage, technical errors, access and activity records, and connection information such as IP address and browser details.
  • Communications: enquiries, support messages, invitation and service-email records, and delivery status. The website contact form collects the information you enter, including your name, email, optional company, and message.

Where you authorize an integration, we receive the selected content and connection credentials or permissions needed for it. Google sign-in alone does not authorize us to read your Google Drive. Do not send passwords, API keys, or payment-card details through the contact form or ordinary email.

How we use it

We use this information to process orders, provision and operate nodes, invite and authenticate users, carry out project instructions, return results, meter and reconcile usage, manage billing, provide support, and send necessary service messages.

We also use relevant records to investigate failures, protect accounts and infrastructure, prevent misuse and fraud, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal and accounting obligations. These uses require different information; for example, project content is processed to perform your work, while transaction records support billing.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, legal obligations, or consent, as applicable. You may withdraw consent for an optional activity without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Where permitted by applicable agreements and law, we may use project material that does not contain personally identifiable information (PII), including material from private projects, to train, fine-tune, or evaluate Sneferu’s own models through processes such as Distillery. We do not use PII for this model-development work. PII means information that identifies, or can reasonably be linked to, an individual.

This distinction concerns Sneferu’s own model development. It does not prevent us from processing personal information to provide your requested service, such as handling your account or carrying out your project instructions. External AI providers’ practices are described below.

Projects, access, and AI processing

A private SOD node does not make your project publicly available by default. Access depends on the node’s accounts, roles, and sharing settings. Authorized administrators, Sneferu personnel providing operations or support, and service providers may process information needed for their functions. “Private” does not mean that nobody except you can ever process the data.

SOD sends relevant prompts, code, files, or other project context to the AI providers selected for the work. Configured providers can include Fireworks AI and Baseten; the providers used depend on the node and model configuration. Their processing, retention, and training terms depend on the provider and applicable account settings. Contact us before submitting material that requires a particular provider, retention period, or data location.

Connecting another application or publishing, exporting, or sharing a result makes the selected information available to that destination. Revoking a connection stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase material already imported, exported, or received by another party. Publicly released material remains subject to its applicable license and the receiving service’s rules.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use providers for website hosting, cloud infrastructure, AI processing, payments, authentication, and communications. Relevant providers include:

  • GitHub Pages for the public website — GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Cloud and Google Workspace for node infrastructure and service email, and Google services for optional sign-in and fonts on some public pages — Google privacy policy.
  • Stripe for hosted checkout, subscriptions, invoices, and billing management — Stripe privacy policy.
  • Formspree for messages submitted through the website contact form — Formspree privacy policy.

Provider links explain those providers’ practices; their general policies are not a promise about the specific contractual controls or settings on your node. We also disclose information at your direction or to your authorized users. We may disclose relevant information when required by law, to protect rights and security, or in a business transaction subject to applicable safeguards.

Providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. We do not promise a particular residency location unless it is expressly agreed for your service. Where applicable law requires a transfer mechanism or other protection, it must be in place for the relevant processing.

Cookies and browser storage

The service uses authentication and session mechanisms to keep account access working. The signup and launching pages use your browser’s session storage to remember checkout details and recover the same order after an interruption. This can include your name, email, promotion code, and order reference. Clearing that storage can remove the local recovery information.

Some public pages load fonts from Google, which causes your browser to contact Google. Stripe and other services you visit may use their own cookies and storage under their policies. Browser controls let you manage cookies and stored data, although blocking necessary storage can affect sign-in or checkout recovery.

Retention and security

We retain information according to its purpose: operating an active account or project, delivering and supporting work, keeping transaction and usage evidence, investigating security issues, and meeting legal obligations. There is no single deletion period for every category. Billing or security records may need to remain after an account closes.

Deleting an upload or project, disconnecting a source, canceling a subscription, and deleting an account are different actions. A cancellation does not automatically erase all project data. Backups, required records, and copies already sent to authorized external services may remain after removal from the active service.

We use safeguards such as access controls, protected connections, password hashing, and encrypted handling of certain stored credentials. No system is completely secure. Keep your password, invitation, and order-management links private, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your choices and rights

You can use the available account, project, connection, export, and billing controls to manage your service. For access, correction, export, deletion, or other privacy requests, email mike@sneferu.ai. We may need to verify your identity and authority before releasing information or changing an organization’s records.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a relevant privacy authority. Some requests are subject to lawful exceptions. We will explain any applicable limitation rather than promise that every record can be erased immediately.

The service is intended for people able to enter a service agreement or authorized to use an organization’s account. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate.

Updates and contact

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes and provide additional notice where required. A new use of private content that requires consent will require that consent.
